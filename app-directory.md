# App Directory submission copy — Hearing Benefits Checker

## App name

Hearing Benefits Checker

## Description (listing card)

Does insurance cover hearing aids? Answer a few questions and see what usually
applies to you — from California Hearing Center.

## Longer description

Wondering whether Medicare, your insurance, or the VA covers hearing aids? The
Hearing Benefits Checker from California Hearing Center asks 6 quick questions
about your coverage and explains in plain language what typically applies to
people with your type of plan — Traditional Medicare, Medicare Advantage,
private insurance, employer plans, VA health care, Medicaid, or self-pay.
General information only, never a coverage promise. You can book a free benefit
check and the practice will verify your specific plan.
